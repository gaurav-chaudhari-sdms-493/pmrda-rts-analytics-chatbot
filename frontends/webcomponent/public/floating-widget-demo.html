Website Integration: PMC AI Floating Chatbot

This page demonstrates the Floating Chatbot Widget embedded directly into the Pune Municipal Corporation public portal.

Embed Code Snippet (Add to your Website HTML):

<!-- 1. Include Vanna Web Component Assets (type="module" required) -->
<script type="module" src="/static/vanna-components.js"></script>

<!-- 2. Include PMC Floating Widget Script -->
<script src="/static/pmc-widget.js"></script>

<!-- 3. Initialize Floating Widget -->
<script>
  PMCWidget.init({
    apiHost: window.location.origin,
    title: "PMC AI Assistant",
    subtitle: "पुणे महानगरपालिका AI सहाय्यक",
    position: "bottom-right",
    primaryColor: "#2563eb"
  });
</script>

👉 Look at the bottom-right corner of your screen! Click the floating blue PMC Chat Circle to open the Floating AI Assistant widget.
